About AI Research Copilot

An advanced academic assistant designed to accelerate literature reviews, paper comprehension, and comparative research.

Deep Document Parsing

Powered by PyMuPDF, extracts full-text, academic headings (Abstract, Methods, Results, Discussion), and maintains precise page offsets for accurate citations.

Instant arXiv Ingestion

Search the arXiv database directly or enter any paper ID (e.g., 2312.00752) to fetch the abstract, authors, and download the full PDF in one click.

ChromaDB Vector Indexing

Chunks are vectorized and indexed into a local, persistent vector database. Queries retrieve semantically relevant context across single or multiple papers.

Grounded Gemini AI Copilot

Leverages Google Gemini 2.5 Flash for high-speed reasoning, LaTeX math formula rendering with KaTeX, and source-grounded answers.

Getting Started

  1. Click "Ingest Paper" in the top bar.
  2. Drag & drop a research PDF or search by arXiv ID.
  3. Once indexed, click "Read & Chat" on any paper card.
  4. Ask questions about methodology, findings, or request paper summaries.
